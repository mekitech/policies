2. İşlenen veriler

Kazamatik varsayılan olarak cihaz üzerinde çalışır. Kaza namazı ve oruç kayıtları, günlük namaz takibi, zikirmatik sayaçları, notlar, mezhep/cinsiyet/dil tercihleri, widget ve bildirim ayarları gibi bilgiler telefonunuzdaki uygulama depolama alanında (Room ve DataStore) tutulur. Android’in otomatik uygulama yedeklemesi devre dışıdır; buluta yalnızca Google ile giriş yapıp yedekleme özelliğini kullandığınızda veri gönderilir.

Namaz/oruç kayıtları, mezhep tercihi ve benzeri ibadet takip verileri KVKK kapsamında özel nitelikli kişisel veri sayılabilecek niteliktedir. Bu veriler varsayılan olarak yalnızca cihazınızda tutulur; bulut yedekleme veya Dua Kardeşliği gibi özellikleri siz açmadıkça sunucularımıza aktarılmaz.

Mezhep, vitir kazasının hesaba katılıp katılmaması, namazın kılınışı, kerahat vakitleri ve tesbihat sırasının seçtiğiniz mezhebe göre gösterilmesi için sorulur. Cinsiyet, günlük namaz takibinde özür günü seçeneğinin açılıp açılmaması için sorulur. İkisi de reklam veya hedefleme için kullanılmaz. Google ile yedek alırsanız bu tercihler yedeğe dahil olur. Kaza namazı hesabı yaptığınızda cinsiyetiniz, Google Play sürümünde kimliğiniz yazılmadan toplam bir sayaca eklenir.

2. Data we process

Kazamatik runs on your device by default. Make-up prayer and fasting records, daily prayer tracking, dhikr counters, notes, madhab/gender/language preferences, widget and notification settings are stored locally (Room and DataStore). Android auto-backup is disabled; data is sent to the cloud only when you sign in with Google and use backup.

Prayer/fasting records, madhab choice, and similar worship-tracking data may qualify as special-category personal data under KVKK. By default this stays on your device only; it is not sent to our servers unless you use cloud backup or Prayer Brotherhood.

Madhab is asked so witr make-up rules, how to pray, kerahat times, and tesbihat order follow the school you select. Gender is asked so the daily prayer tracker can show or hide the excused-day option. Neither is used for advertising or targeting. If you back up with Google, these preferences are included. When you save a make-up prayer calculation, gender is added to an aggregate counter on the Google Play build, without your identity.

The following is processed only when you use the related feature:

Data types by feature
  • Google account: For backup, restore, and Prayer Brotherhood, your email, display name, and uid are used for authentication. Backup data is not sent to your inbox or Google Drive as a file; it is stored on Firebase servers linked only to your account.
  • Prayer times: Your selected country/city/district is stored on device; timetables are fetched from ezanvakti.imsakiyem.com and cached locally. Name and email are not sent.
  • Location: On Qibla, nearby mosques, or location picker screens, a one-time/current fix is used; no continuous background tracking. Nearby mosques may send coordinates to Overpass/OpenStreetMap services.
  • Support: Name, email, message, ANDROID_ID, brand/model, Android version, app version, and language from Help are sent to Firebase servers.
  • Crash logs: Firebase Crashlytics on Google Play builds; AGConnect Crash on AppGallery builds; technical logs, installation id, and connection records such as IP may be collected.
  • Notifications: Adhan alarms, worship reminders, and widget alerts use notification permission and local scheduling on your device.
  • Backup: After Google sign-in, manual backup stores make-up prayer and fasting debts and sessions, daily prayer marks, voluntary prayer records, notes, saved locations, dhikr counters and custom dhikr, chain-keeping records, Quran radio favorites, madhab, gender, language and other preferences, and device brand/model in Firebase Realtime Database (Google infrastructure), linked to your account. The backup does not include audio files or photos. Nothing is sent to Gmail or Drive; restore is only through the app.
  • Prayer Brotherhood: After Google sign-in, your prayer title, description, category, and (unless anonymous) display name are stored on Firebase; amin records are linked to your uid.
  • Anonymous statistics: On the Google Play build, aggregate prayer/fasting calculation counts (by gender) and saved district counts are written to Firebase without personal identity. The AppGallery build does not send these counters.
  • Usage analytics: Screen views via Google Analytics (Play) or Huawei HiAnalytics (AppGallery) for app improvement; no opt-out is offered.
  • Push infrastructure: An FCM or Huawei Push token may be stored on your device so notification channels can work.
What we do not collect
  • Selling or renting your data
  • Cross-app behavioral tracking
  • Uploading contacts, microphone recordings, or camera images to our servers
  • Continuous background location tracking

3. Purposes and legal bases

We do not sell or rent your data. Processing is limited to:

  • Running make-up tracking, prayer times, widgets, dhikr, duas, notes, and your preferences
  • Delivering notifications and alarms you have enabled
  • Answering support requests and showing prior threads on the same device
  • Detecting and fixing crashes, and monitoring version / device compatibility
  • Carrying out optional backup, restore, and Prayer Brotherhood services
Legal bases under KVKK (summary)
  • Non-special on-device data such as language, widgets, and notification settings: performance of the service (KVKK Art. 5/2-c). Madhab, gender, and worship records: explicit consent (KVKK Art. 6). Accepting the policy in onboarding and saving the related preference counts as consent.
  • Google backup and Prayer Brotherhood: explicit consent (KVKK Art. 5/1; Art. 6 for special-category data included in backup). Enabling these features constitutes consent.
  • Support messages: performance of the service and legitimate interest (replying to requests)
  • Crash logs and anonymous statistics: legitimate interest (security and improvement)
  • Usage analytics: legitimate interest (app improvement)

Providers and transferred data
